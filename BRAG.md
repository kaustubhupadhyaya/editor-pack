# BRAG

## What the video shows

`brag-output/brag.mp4` (289,573 bytes) is a 20-second, 1920x1080, 25 fps H.264 clip with a silent AAC track. It holds
one static poster for the full 20 seconds. It is not an animated demo, a screen recording, or a rendered reel.

The poster reads:

- "LAUNCH BRIEF"
- **editor-pack**
- "Modular video editing skills engine and offline reel processor with frame-exact cuts and audio synchronization."
- Engineering highlights: frame-exact beat alignment and speed-ramping presets; offline audio and visual reel content
  processor; automated FFmpeg assembly pipeline for batch reel delivery.
- Footer: "Beat-synced. Frame-exact. Automated video editing at scale." and "PRODUCTION READY".

## The "without Premiere" claim is not verified

The creative plan behind this card says "offline multi-stem processing without Premiere crashes". Nothing in this repo
benchmarks editor-pack against Premiere, and no crash comparison or multi-stem test is recorded. The README describes
a skills library and makes no Premiere-parity claim. Treat "without Premiere" as positioning, not a measured result.
